Prompt Leaf

A document becomes a command line: the most direct possible combination of Confluence content and CLI access.

What works

It tells the product story in one beat: a page that can be read or addressed as a command. The mark has excellent favicon clarity, simple geometry, and effortless monochrome reproduction.

Honest risk

Document-and-prompt marks exist elsewhere. Distinction must come from protecting this exact leaf silhouette, the unusually deep fold, and the compact prompt rhythm.

Open Channel

Two leaves form one open surface: prose on the left, a command prompt on the right, and a narrow green spine joining human and machine use.

What works

The silhouette reads first as open content, then rewards a closer look with the scripting cue. It is broad, compact, ownable, and not dependent on a safety metaphor.

Without the wordmark it can suggest technical publishing or developer documentation. Its custom proportions and split-color spine need consistent protection.

Choose Prompt Leaf.

It compresses the complete product proposition into the fastest-reading mark in the set: Confluence content, made directly available at the command line. That clarity serves first-time users without sacrificing the compactness developers expect.

  1. It explains the product immediately. The page says human-readable knowledge; the prompt says scriptable access for people and agents.
  2. It avoids the verification trap. There is no checkmark, shield, or sync loop. The emphasis is access to content—not policing it.
  3. It survives reality. The strong outer silhouette and short prompt remain coherent at 16 px, in one color, and without the wordmark.
  4. It fits the voice. “Confluence, readable and scriptable.” maps cleanly onto the two ingredients inside the mark.

Watch-out: resist making the page generic. Preserve the deep folded corner, the slightly compressed proportions, and the exact prompt placement; those details turn a familiar vocabulary into a recognizable signature.
